Services

  • Local one-way: a verified driver drives the customer’s own car for a single city journey.
  • Local hourly / round trip: book a driver for a selected time package in the customer’s own car; pickup and return location are the same.
  • Outstation one-way: requests up to 500 km use the published distance bands; kilometres driven beyond the accepted quote are ₹10/km. Longer routes need Admin review.
  • Outstation round trip: use day packages from 1 to 11 days, with no kilometre charge; extra duty time is ₹100/hour.

DrivX does not provide a taxi/cab fleet or a vehicle to the customer. The customer supplies the car. Customers pay the agreed ride fare in cash directly to the driver after the ride. Cashfree checkout is used for the driver’s one-time onboarding fee and eligible driver platform dues; it is not used to collect customer ride fares.

  • DrivX connects customers to a verified driver who drives the customer’s own car. The customer is responsible for providing a roadworthy car and valid vehicle documents.

  • Final trip distance is checked using start and end odometer readings. Any applicable extra distance or overtime is calculated under the package rules and shown in the final bill.

Outstation round trips use day packages through 11 days; overtime is ₹100/hour after package duty hours. Local round trips return to the selected pickup and are priced by time only. Any taxes or extras are shown only when configured and disclosed in the booking quote.

Refunds & Cancellations

Customer bookings

A customer may cancel before the trip starts. No cancellation fee is currently charged. Once a trip has started, contact support promptly if there is a problem. Ride fares are paid in cash directly to the driver; DrivX does not collect or process in-app ride-fare refunds.

Driver onboarding fee

The one-time onboarding fee (₹500 plus 5% GST by default) is refundable if DrivX rejects the driver application. The refund is returned to the original payment method after the application decision; payment-provider processing time may apply. If an applicant withdraws before a decision, contact support for a case review; withdrawal does not automatically qualify for a refund.

Driver dues payments

Cashfree payments toward driver platform dues are applied only after gateway confirmation. If a payment is debited but the DrivX due remains unpaid, contact support with the Cashfree order/payment reference so the transaction can be checked. Duplicate or failed payments are handled after provider verification and refunded where applicable.

Privacy Policy

DrivX collects information needed to provide driver-booking and driver-onboarding services. This may include a verified mobile number, name, email, pickup/drop-off details, booking and odometer records, support messages, driver identity/licence documents, payment references, and location while a driver is online or completing an active trip.

How information is used

  • To sign in, create and secure customer/driver accounts, process bookings, calculate fares and dues, and provide trip updates.
  • To verify driver applications, review uploaded documents, collect the configured onboarding fee and driver dues through Cashfree, and keep payment/audit records.
  • To share the minimum trip details needed between the assigned driver and customer, provide support, prevent misuse, and meet legal/accounting requirements.

Service providers and retention

DrivX uses service providers for hosting, authentication, maps, messaging and payment processing, including Firebase/Google services and Cashfree. Payment credentials are handled by the payment provider; DrivX stores payment/order references and status, not full card credentials. Driver live location is shared for an active trip or availability functions and is not intended as continuous idle tracking. Records are retained while needed to operate the service, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations, then deleted or de-identified where appropriate.
