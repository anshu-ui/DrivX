DrivX Captain · Driver privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how DrivX handles information when you apply to drive, use the Captain app, go online, accept a booking, complete a trip, or contact support.

Effective date: 22 November 2026

In short: DrivX uses your profile and verification documents to review and support your driver account. Precise location is used while you are online and during an assigned active trip. The customer assigned to that trip may see trip tracking and the driver details needed for the booking.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the parts of DrivX Captain you use, we may process:

  • Account and application details: your mobile number used for sign-in, name and required email address, driver account/reference, verification and application status, languages and work zone. You or the DrivX team may add your vehicle make, model or number plate.
  • Verification documents: the profile photograph, Aadhaar card, driving licence and PAN card that you upload for review. Aadhaar, licence and PAN are uploaded as front and reverse images; the profile photograph is a single face photo. The images can contain full document numbers. The structured document record stores a masked number, but that does not remove the number visible in the original image.
  • Precise location: GPS coordinates, accuracy, timestamp and, when available, heading and speed. This is collected while you are online for dispatch and while an assigned booking is in a trackable trip state. Background location updates can continue when the app is not on screen, subject to your device permissions.
  • Booking and trip records: booking reference, pickup and destination, requested time, instructions or notes relevant to the trip, trip status, odometer readings, estimated and final fare, and trip timing.
  • Earnings and payment records: commissions and other configured dues, onboarding fee, payment amount, Cashfree order/payment reference and confirmed payment status, as applicable.
  • Support, ratings and app information: support messages and replies, driver ratings and trip counts, notification device token if you enable push alerts, and basic app screen/technical information used to operate and improve the service.

2. Documents and profile visibility

Your uploaded identity and licence documents are stored for DrivX verification and are restricted to your account and authorized DrivX staff who need them to review or support your application. Customers are not shown these document images or the numbers on them.

Your profile photograph is copied separately for driver-card display. When a customer is assigned to you, the trip may show your name, phone number, photograph, driver reference, vehicle details, and available rating/trip-count information so the customer can identify and contact the assigned driver. The profile-photo copy is available to signed-in DrivX users; it is not the same as giving customers access to your identity or licence documents.

3. Location and background access

When you go online, the Captain app sends your precise location to DrivX to show your availability in dispatch and help match nearby bookings. This can happen even before you accept a trip. While an assigned booking is in a trackable state, location updates support trip coordination and may be shown to that booking’s customer in the trip-tracking view. Location is not shown to unrelated customers.

The app may keep collecting location in the background while you remain online or while trip tracking is active, as allowed by the location permission you grant and your phone’s operating system. Going offline stops availability updates; location may continue for an active assigned trip until trip tracking ends. If you disable location permission, online dispatch or trip features may not work. You can manage location access in your device settings and go offline in the app when you are not available for work.

4. Trips, earnings and payments

DrivX keeps trip and ledger records to operate the driver service, show your completed trips and earnings, calculate the commission or other dues configured for a trip, and resolve payment or trip questions. The customer’s fare is paid directly to the driver; DrivX’s in-app Cashfree checkout is for driver onboarding and eligible driver-platform dues, not customer ride fares.

Cashfree provides the checkout flow. DrivX creates an order and receives payment/order references and gateway-confirmed status so it can update the relevant due. Payment details you enter in the gateway checkout are also subject to Cashfree’s privacy terms. Do not send card details, passwords or one-time codes to DrivX support.

5. How we use information

  • Sign you in, secure your account and administer driver onboarding and document review.
  • Show your availability to dispatch, send eligible booking offers and coordinate an assigned trip.
  • Provide customers with the driver and vehicle details needed for their assigned booking.
  • Record trip completion, odometer readings, fares, commission, dues and confirmed payments.
  • Send booking and service notifications when you enable them, answer support requests, investigate disputes or safety reports, and maintain service reliability.
  • Meet applicable legal, accounting and record-keeping obligations.

6. When information is shared

  • Assigned customer: the driver and vehicle details needed to recognize and contact you, plus live location during that assigned booking’s trackable trip state.
  • Authorized DrivX staff: account, documents, location, booking, support and payment records as needed for verification, dispatch, support, safety, reconciliation or dispute handling.
  • Service providers: providers that host the app, process sign-in and data, support maps, analytics, notifications or payments.
  • Legal and safety purposes: information may be disclosed when required by law or reasonably needed to protect people, investigate misuse or resolve a safety issue.

DrivX does not sell your personal information.

7. Service providers and analytics

DrivX uses Google Firebase services for parts of sign-in, hosting, database, storage and push messaging; Google services for maps/location-related features and analytics; and Cashfree for driver onboarding and driver-platform dues payments. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own privacy terms.

The app records fixed screen labels for basic usage analytics. The screen event is not designed to include your booking ID or text you enter. The app may also process technical information needed for operation and reliability. Push alerts use a device token when you enable them; you can turn off notifications in the app or your device settings.

8. Retention and security

We keep your profile and documents while needed to maintain and review your driver account. If an application is rejected or withdrawn, we may retain its decision and review history. Trip, earnings, payment, support and audit records may be kept for as long as needed for service operations, reconciliation, disputes, safety and applicable legal or accounting requirements. Retention periods can differ by record type; we delete or de-identify records when they are no longer needed and we are allowed to do so.

Access controls and other safeguards are used to protect the information we hold. No online service can promise absolute security, so keep sign-in codes private and contact DrivX promptly if you believe your account or documents were accessed improperly.

9. Your choices and requests

You can update profile information available in the app, manage location and notification permissions in your phone settings, and contact us to ask for access to or correction of your account information. Turning off location can prevent you from going online or using trip features.

Account deletion is not an automatic in-app action today. To request deletion or ask a privacy question, open Help/Support in the Captain app and submit a request, or email customersupport@drivx.in. We will verify that you control the driver account and handle the request. Some trip, payment, safety, dispute or audit records may need to be retained for the reasons above; where retained, we limit their use to those purposes.
