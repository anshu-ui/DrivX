Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Whose car does the driver use?

The service provides a professional driver for the customer’s own car.

How do I pay for my ride?

Customer ride fares are paid in cash directly to the driver after the trip.

Are all outstation trips fixed-price?

Outstation one-way bands are available through 500 km; outstation round trips use day packages through 11 days.

How do drivers start working?

Drivers submit their details and required documents, pay the one-time ₹500 onboarding fee + 5% GST (₹525 total by default) through Cashfree, and wait for Admin verification. If DrivX rejects an application, the fee is refundable to the original payment method.

How do drivers pay platform dues?

Applicable platform commission and other dues appear in the driver account. Drivers can review and pay outstanding dues through Cashfree. The payment is recorded after gateway confirmation.
